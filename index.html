Smartvel content can support Swedavia across destination pages, airport route discovery, Travel Steps, email marketing, pre-departure communications and destination inspiration. The strongest value comes when the same destination content is reused across several passenger touchpoints.

Solution 1 · Destination inspiration + route discovery

Explore & Go

Explore & Go transforms static destination lists into interactive discovery experiences. Passengers can browse destinations visually, filter by interests, and understand where they can travel from a selected airport or route network.

Interactive discoveryMaps, filters and visual destination cards replace passive destination lists.
Airport-specific viewsExperiences can be configured around agreed airports, origins and destination sets.
Campaign-readySupports seasonal routes, new route launches and destination marketing.
Intent generationMoves passengers from passive browsing towards destination consideration.

How Swedavia could use Explore & Go

The strongest Swedavia use case is visualising destination choice from each airport: a passenger selects an airport, explores available destinations, filters by interest or travel type, and opens destination content directly from the map or list.

  • Show destinations served from selected Swedavia airports.
  • Support airport-level route showcases without implying every airport must launch at once.
  • Highlight seasonal routes, charter routes and campaign destinations.
  • Use direct-route logic, origin logic and interest filters where technically scoped.
  • Connect destination inspiration to booking, route pages or campaign CTAs.

Solution 2 · Destination engagement + itinerary intelligence

Trip Planner

Trip Planner turns destination content into an active planning experience. It combines POIs, restaurants, live events, destination cards and itinerary tools so passengers can explore what to do before and during travel.

POIs & restaurantsUseful places, attractions, food, culture and local highlights.
Live eventsConcerts, exhibitions, sport, festivals and activities linked to travel dates.
Itinerary builderHelp travellers organise places and events into a practical destination plan.
Partner-ready CTAsCan link to booking flows, ancillary services or third-party providers where agreed.

Where Trip Planner fits for Swedavia

Trip Planner becomes most relevant after a passenger has chosen, searched or been shown a destination. It gives Swedavia deeper content for destination pages, Travel Steps and pre-trip inspiration.

  • Support “what to do in Stockholm / Gothenburg / destination city” content journeys.
  • Provide stopover and short-stay inspiration where relevant.
  • Surface live events and timely activities around travel dates.
  • Connect destination content to airport services, partners or booking CTAs.
  • Keep passengers engaged beyond the route or destination listing.
